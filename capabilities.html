Common design considerations.

What finishes can be considered?

Depending on substrate and supplier, options may include foil, embossing, specialty coatings and textured papers.

How should materials be selected?

Consider product protection, weight, assembly, regulatory requirements and the intended disposal pathway.

When are prototypes useful?

Prototypes can help review dimensions, fit, opening experience and display stability before production.
